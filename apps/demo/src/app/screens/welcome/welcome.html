A guided breathing exercise

With your permission, this page uses your camera to estimate your heart rate and your breathing rate while you follow a slow breathing pattern.

  • No recording: video frames are analysed and discarded at once.
  • Nothing leaves this device: nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.
  • No identification: the face tracker finds where your skin is, not who you are.

Not a medical device. These are wellness estimates for a breathing exercise, not a diagnosis.
